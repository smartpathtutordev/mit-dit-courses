## How it works

```

tools/engine/               shared player = W1D1 design (base.css copied from W1D1) + new activity types
tools/build.py              lesson -> SPT/ENG/GRADE1/Q1/WEEKX/DAYY/INTERACTIVE/index.html
                            + speech/_lines.json (every voice line) + SCRIPT.md + IMAGE_PROMPTS.md
                            + PLAY-LESSONS.html (menu)   — and enforces the rules below
tools/tts_gemini.py         makes speech/*.mp3 with Gemini preview TTS (Leda)
tools/playtest.js           NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node tools/playtest.js [--shots DIR]
```

## The 6-year-old rules (enforced by build.py)

- 10–18 slides; cover first; Bible verse then celebration last (school is faith-based; keep the day's verse).
- 2–3 goals on the cover, ≤7 words each, written as "I can…" child actions; every goal tagged on ≥1 slide.
- ≥6 hands-on slides, ≥2 understanding checks (`pick`/`order`), never >3 listen-only slides in a row
  (put a quick `pick` in the middle of a story).
- Teacher sentences ≤14 words, ≤75 words per slide. Card words ≤4 words, meanings ≤10 words.
- No phonetic spellings like `[dih-SKUH-ver-ee]`, no pop-ups, no "wrong" — wrong taps wobble and give a hint.
- Listen first, then "Your turn!"; every tap = voice + star; Next glows when the slide is done.
- Pictures must exist and match the word.

  mapped, so the rebuilt lessons do not use them yet. FIRST TODO: contact-sheet those PNGs and, where they match,

## Per-lesson plan (what each lesson must do)

Every lesson follows the same arc (≈10–12 min):
**cover (goals) → teach 3–5 words with pictures (cards) → move/act or chant → listen-and-tap check →
short story in 2–4 picture pages with a mid-story question → order/pick comprehension check →
child's own sentence (sentence builder) → "your turn to talk" frame → verse → celebration recap.**
