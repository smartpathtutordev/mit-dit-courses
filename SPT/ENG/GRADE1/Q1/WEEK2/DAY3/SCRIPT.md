# Naming Words for People

## Objectives (the child will...)

1. I can name my family
2. I can sing a family song
3. I can tell special names

### 3. More Family Words — cards (Tala)

- tap **grandpa** → "Grandpa. (pause) Lolo. (pause) He is the father of my mom or dad."

### 4. Listen and Tap! — pick (Tala)

### 5. The Family Song — chant (Tala)

### 12. Common Names — cards (Tala)
> Every person has two kinds of naming words. (pause) Mom, dad, sister, brother. (pause) These are common names. (pause) Many people are called mom! (pause) We write them with small letters.
- tap **mom** → "mom. (pause) Small letter m."
- tap **dad** → "dad. (pause) Small letter d."
- tap **sister** → "sister. (pause) Small letter s."
- tap **brother** → "brother. (pause) Small letter b."

### 13. Special Names — cards (Tala)
> Mom is a common name. But my mom has her own special name! (pause) Now, special names! (pause) A special name is for ONE person. (pause) It starts with a big capital letter.
- tap **Lita** → "Lita. (pause) My mom’s name is Lita. (pause) Big letter L."
- tap **Rico** → "Rico. (pause) My dad’s name is Rico. (pause) Big letter R."
- tap **Hinhin** → "Hinhin. (pause) My sister’s name is Hinhin. (pause) Big letter H."
- tap **Gas** → "Gas. (pause) My brother’s name is Gas. (pause) Big letter G."

### 14. Common or Special? — pick (Tala)
> Let us check: common or special? (pause) Same person, two names! (pause) Listen and tap.
- check: Tap the SPECIAL name. → **Hinhin**
- check: Which starts with a BIG letter? → **Rico**
- check: Tap the COMMON name. → **mom**

### 15. This Is My... — sentence (Tala)
> You know common and special names. Now build a sentence! (pause) Now show your family! (pause) Tap a naming word. (pause) Then say the sentence with me.
- tap **mom** → "This is my mom."
- tap **dad** → "This is my dad."
- tap **grandma** → "This is my grandma."
- tap **older brother** → "This is my older brother."

### 16. Tell Me About Your Family! — talk (Tala)
