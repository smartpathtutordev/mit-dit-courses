# Listen, Follow, and Ask

Grade 1 English · Quarter 1 • Week 2 • Day 2 · `g1q1w2d2`

## Objectives (the child will...)

1. Follow what I hear
2. Use who, what, where
3. Ask about a story

Source: LANG1Q1W2DAY2LESSON — Following Instructions & Asking Questions (Proverbs 22:6)

## Slides

### 1. Listen, Follow, and Ask — cover (Kuya Gas)
> Hello! I am Kuya Gas! (pause) Today you learn two big skills. (pause) First, how to follow directions. (pause) Second, how to ask questions!

### 2. Action Words — cards (Kuya Gas)
> These are action words. (pause) Tap each one. (pause) Then do the action!
- tap **stand** → "Stand! (pause) Stand up tall."
- tap **sit** → "Sit! (pause) Sit down."
- tap **clap** → "Clap! (pause) Clap your hands."
- tap **smile** → "Smile! (pause) Give a big smile."
- tap **wave** → "Wave! (pause) Wave hello."
- tap **bow** → "Bow! (pause) Bow your head."

### 3. How to Play Simon Says — story (Kuya Gas)
> Let us play Simon Says! (pause) If I say, Simon says, (pause) do the action. (pause) If I do not say Simon says, (pause) stay still! (pause) Listen carefully!

### 4. Simon Says! — act (Kuya Gas)
> Stand up! Ready? (pause) Listen to each one. (pause) Then tap what you did.
- tap **Stand!** → "Simon says, Stand!"
- tap **Clap!** → "Simon says, Clap!"
- tap **Smile!** → "Smile!"
- tap **Bow!** → "Simon says, Bow!"
- tap **Sit!** → "Sit!"
- tap **Wave!** → "Simon says, Wave!"

### 5. Question Words — cards (Kuya Gas)
> Now, question words! (pause) We use them to ask. (pause) Tap each one.
- tap **Who?** → "Who. (pause) Who is for a person. (pause) Who is your friend?"
- tap **What?** → "What. (pause) What is for a thing. (pause) What is your toy?"
- tap **Where?** → "Where. (pause) Where is for a place. (pause) Where is your school?"

### 6. Which Question Word? — pick (Kuya Gas)
> Which question word do we use? (pause) Listen and tap!
- check: For a PERSON, we ask... → **Who?**
- check: For a PLACE, we ask... → **Where?**
- check: For a THING, we ask... → **What?**

### 7. Katkat the Kitten — story (Kuya Gas)
> Story time! (pause) Listen, and think of questions. (pause) This is Katkat. (pause) He is a little orange kitten. (pause) He lives near the beach.

### 8. Katkat the Kitten — story (Kuya Gas)
> On Saturday morning, Katkat looks for his ball. (pause) His ball has a bell. (pause) Oh no! (pause) It is missing! (pause) Where is it?

### 9. Katkat the Kitten — story (Kuya Gas)
> Katkat looks in the garden, near the mango tree. (pause) A white kitten has his ball! (pause) She gives it back. (pause) Now they are friends!

### 10. Ask About the Story! — pick (Kuya Gas)
> Let us ask questions about the story. (pause) Listen to each question!
- check: WHO is the story about? → **Katkat**
- check: WHAT did Katkat lose? → **his ball**
- check: WHERE did he find it? → **in the garden**

### 11. Now YOU Ask a Question! — sentence (Kuya Gas)
> Now you ask! (pause) Tap a question. (pause) Then ask someone at home.
- tap **Who is your best friend?** → "Who is your best friend?"
- tap **What is your favorite toy?** → "What is your favorite toy?"
- tap **Where do you want to go?** → "Where do you want to go?"

### 12. Answer Like Katkat! — talk (Kuya Gas)
> Now answer a question. (pause) What is your favorite toy? (pause) Say: My favorite toy is my ball.
