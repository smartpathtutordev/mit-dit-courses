# Listen, Follow, and Ask

Grade 1 English · Quarter 1 • Week 2 • Day 2 · `g1q1w2d2`

## Objectives (the child will...)

1. Follow what I hear
2. Use who, what, where
3. Ask about a story

Source: LANG1Q1W2DAY2LESSON — Following Instructions & Asking Questions (Proverbs 22:6)

## Slides

### 2. Action Words — cards (Kuya Gas)

- tap **stand** → "Stand! (pause) Stand up tall."
- tap **sit** → "Sit! (pause) Sit down."
- tap **clap** → "Clap! (pause) Clap your hands."
- tap **smile** → "Smile! (pause) Give a big smile."
- tap **wave** → "Wave! (pause) Wave hello."
- tap **bow** → "Bow! (pause) Bow your head."

### 3. How to Play Simon Says — story (Kuya Gas)
> You know the actions! Now let us use them in a game. (pause) Let us play Simon Says! (pause) If I say, Simon says, (pause) do the action. (pause) If I do not say Simon says, (pause) stay still! (pause) Listen carefully!

### 4. Simon Says! — act (Kuya Gas)
> Ready to play? (pause) Stand up! Ready? (pause) Listen to each one. (pause) Then tap what you did.
- tap **Stand!** → "Simon says, Stand!"
- tap **Clap!** → "Simon says, Clap!"
- tap **Smile!** → "Smile!"
- tap **Bow!** → "Simon says, Bow!"

### 5. Question Words — cards (Kuya Gas)
> Great listening! Now, the second skill: asking questions. (pause) Now, question words! (pause) We use them to ask. (pause) Tap each one.
- tap **Who?** → "Who. (pause) Who is for a person. (pause) Who is your friend?"
- tap **What?** → "What. (pause) What is for a thing. (pause) What is your toy?"
- tap **Where?** → "Where. (pause) Where is for a place. (pause) Where is your school?"

### 6. Which Question Word? — pick (Kuya Gas)
> Let us practice the question words. (pause) Which question word do we use? (pause) Listen and tap!
- check: For a PERSON, we ask... → **Who?**
- check: For a PLACE, we ask... → **Where?**
- check: For a THING, we ask... → **What?**

### 7. Katkat the Kitten — story (Kuya Gas)
> Now listen to a story. Think of questions you can ask! (pause) Story time! (pause) Listen, and think of questions. (pause) This is Katkat. (pause) He is a little orange kitten. (pause) He lives near the beach.

### 8. Katkat the Kitten — story (Kuya Gas)
> What happens to Katkat? (pause) On Saturday morning, Katkat looks for his ball. (pause) His ball has a bell. (pause) Oh no! (pause) It is missing! (pause) Where is it?

### 9. How Does Katkat Feel? — pick (Kuya Gas)
> Poor Katkat. (pause) His ball is missing. (pause) How does Katkat feel?
- check: Katkat feels... → **sad**

### 10. Katkat the Kitten — story (Kuya Gas)
> Let us help Katkat look! (pause) Katkat looks everywhere. (pause) He looks in the garden. (pause) He looks near the mango tree. (pause) Look! (pause) Who is that?

### 11. Katkat the Kitten — story (Kuya Gas)
> Look! Who is that? (pause) Katkat looks in the garden, near the mango tree. (pause) A white kitten has his ball! (pause) She gives it back. (pause) Now they are friends!

### 12. Ask About the Story! — pick (Kuya Gas)
> That is the end of the story. Now let us ask questions about it! (pause) Let us ask questions about the story. (pause) Listen to each question!
- check: WHO is the story about? → **Katkat**
- check: WHAT did Katkat lose? → **his ball**
