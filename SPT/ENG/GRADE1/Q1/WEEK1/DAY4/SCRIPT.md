# My Body, Many Languages

Grade 1 English · Quarter 1 • Week 1 • Day 4 · `g1q1w1d4`

## Objectives (the child will...)

1. Name parts of my body
2. Play Touch Your Body
3. Learn: many languages!

Source: LANG1Q1W1DAY4NARRATIONSCRIPT — Body Parts and Language Diversity (Exodus 20:12)

## Slides

### 1. My Body, Many Languages — cover (Tala)
> Good morning, my friend! (pause) This is the last day of our first week. (pause) Today we learn the parts of our body. (pause) And I have a surprise for you!

### 2. My Head and Face — cards (Tala)
> First, let us learn the parts of our head and face. (pause) Let us learn five words. (pause) Tap each picture. (pause) Then touch that part of your face!
- tap **head** → "Head. (pause) Ulo. (pause) Touch your head!"
- tap **eyes** → "Eyes. (pause) Mata. (pause) Blink your eyes!"
- tap **ears** → "Ears. (pause) Tenga. (pause) Touch your ears!"
- tap **nose** → "Nose. (pause) Ilong. (pause) Touch your nose!"
- tap **mouth** → "Mouth. (pause) Bibig. (pause) Smile with your mouth!"

### 3. My Body — cards (Tala)
> You know your face! Now let us go down to the rest of the body. (pause) Now five more words! (pause) Tap each picture. (pause) Then move that part of your body!
- tap **shoulders** → "Shoulders. (pause) Balikat. (pause) Touch your shoulders!"
- tap **hands** → "Hands. (pause) Kamay. (pause) Clap your hands!"
- tap **tummy** → "Tummy. (pause) Tiyan. (pause) Pat your tummy!"
- tap **knees** → "Knees. (pause) Tuhod. (pause) Touch your knees!"
- tap **feet** → "Feet. (pause) Paa. (pause) Stamp your feet!"

### 4. Sing and Touch! — chant (Tala)
> Ten body words! Let us put them in a song. (pause) Let us sing! (pause) Stand up. (pause) Touch each part as you sing!
- tap **Head, shoulders, knees and feet,** → "Head, shoulders, knees and feet!"
- tap **knees and feet!** → "Knees and feet!"
- tap **Eyes and ears and mouth and nose,** → "Eyes and ears and mouth and nose!"
- tap **Paa, tuhod, balikat, ulo!** → "Paa, tuhod, balikat, ulo!"

### 5. Touch Your Body! — act (Tala)
> Great singing! Now let us play a game. (pause) Let us play a game! (pause) I say a word. (pause) You touch that part. (pause) Ready?
- tap **Touch your head!** → "Touch your head!"
- tap **Touch your feet!** → "Touch your feet!"
- tap **Blink your eyes!** → "Blink your eyes!"
- tap **Touch your knees!** → "Touch your knees!"
- tap **Pat your tummy!** → "Pat your tummy!"
- tap **Touch your ears!** → "Touch your ears!"

### 6. Listen and Tap! — pick (Tala)
> Super moving! Now let us use our eyes and ears. (pause) Listen carefully. (pause) Tap the right picture!
- check: Tap the nose! → **nose**
- check: Tap the hands! → **hands**

### 7. Different Words, Same Meaning! — langs (Tala)
> You know the words in English and Tagalog. Here is my surprise! (pause) Here is my surprise! (pause) Our country has many languages. (pause) Listen to the word for ears. (pause) Tap each card!
- tap **tenga** → "In Tagalog, we say tenga."
- tap **dalunggan** → "In Cebuano, they say dalunggan."
- tap **lapayag** → "In Ilocano, they say lapayag."
- tap **balugbug** → "In Kapampangan, they say balugbug."

### 8. Think About It! — pick (Tala)
> Four words, one meaning! (pause) Let us think!
- check: Tenga, dalunggan, lapayag. They all mean... → **ears**
- check: A friend says a different word. We... → **listen**

### 9. What Do You Say at Home? — talk (Tala)
> Every family has its own words. What about yours? (pause) What language do you speak at home? (pause) What do you call your ears at home? (pause) Say: At home, I say tenga. (pause) Use your own word!

### 10. All Languages Are Beautiful — story (Tala)
> Thank you for sharing your word! (pause) Look at our country, the Philippines. (pause) People speak many languages. (pause) Every language is beautiful. (pause) And your language is beautiful too!
