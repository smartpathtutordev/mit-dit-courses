# Easy lesson script

**Day 1: Myself and my family**

Short Easy path. No hello. Objective first, then tap, then a tiny check.

## Path

1. **today** (hello)
   - Sage: Ready? Today is YOU and your family!

2. **words** (words)
   - Sage: Tap a word! Say it loud!

3. **teach-i** (teach)
   - Sage: Look! I. I means me. Say I!
   - Word: I — I means me.

4. **teach-name** (teach)
   - Sage: One more! NAME. A name is what people call you. Say NAME!
   - Word: NAME — A name is what people call you.

5. **try** (pair)
   - Sage: Your turn! Tap one. Shout it!

6. **check** (check)
   - Sage: Quiz time! Tap the right one.

7. **bye** (verse)
   - Sage: You did it! Great job!
