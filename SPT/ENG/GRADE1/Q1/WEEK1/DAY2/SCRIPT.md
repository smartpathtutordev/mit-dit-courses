# Getting Ready for School

Grade 1 English · Quarter 1 • Week 1 • Day 2 · `g1q1w1d2`

## Objectives (the child will...)

1. Say 5 morning words
2. Tell a story in order
3. Talk about MY morning

Source: LANG1Q1W1DAY2NARRATIONSCRIPT — Preparing for School (Exodus 20:12)

## Slides

### 1. Getting Ready for School — cover (Tala)
> Good morning, my friend! (pause) I am Tala. (pause) Yesterday we talked about you and your family. (pause) Today, we get ready for school!

### 2. 5 Morning Words — cards (Tala)
> First, let us learn our morning words. (pause) Every morning, we do five things. (pause) Listen. (pause) Then tap each picture and say the words with me!
- tap **wake up** → "Wake up. (pause) We open our eyes."
- tap **take a bath** → "Take a bath. (pause) We use soap and water."
- tap **get dressed** → "Get dressed. (pause) We put on our clothes."
- tap **eat breakfast** → "Eat breakfast. (pause) Our first food of the day."
- tap **brush teeth** → "Brush your teeth. (pause) Clean and shiny!"

### 3. Show Me! Act It Out! — act (Tala)
> You know the five words! Now let us move our bodies. (pause) Let us play! (pause) Stand up. (pause) I say the words. (pause) You act them out!
- tap **Wake up!** → "Wake up! (pause) Stretch your arms up high!"
- tap **Take a bath!** → "Take a bath! (pause) Scrub, scrub, scrub!"
- tap **Get dressed!** → "Get dressed! (pause) Button your shirt."
- tap **Eat breakfast!** → "Eat breakfast! (pause) Yum, yum!"
- tap **Brush your teeth!** → "Brush your teeth! (pause) Up and down, up and down."

### 4. Listen and Tap! — pick (Tala)
> Great acting! Now let us use our ears. (pause) Now, listen carefully. (pause) I say a word. (pause) You tap the right picture.
- check: Tap: brush teeth → **brush teeth**
- check: Tap: eat breakfast → **eat breakfast**
- check: Tap: take a bath → **take a bath**

### 5. Story: Before Going to School — story (Tala)
> You know the morning words. Now let us see them in a story! (pause) Story time! (pause) Our story is called, Before Going to School. (pause) This is Tala and Bunso. (pause) They wake up early. (pause) It is time for school!

### 6. Story: Before Going to School — story (Tala)
> What do Tala and Bunso do next? (pause) Nanay says, hurry up! (pause) They take a bath. (pause) They get dressed. (pause) They eat breakfast. (pause) Then they brush their teeth.

### 7. Quick! What Did They Do? — pick (Tala)
> They did so many things! (pause) Quick question!
- check: After the bath, they... → **got dressed**

### 8. Story: Before Going to School — story (Tala)
> They are almost ready for school. (pause) But wait. (pause) Oh no! (pause) Bunso's pencil is missing! (pause) Tala's crayons are missing! (pause) Where can they be?

### 9. How Do They Feel? — pick (Tala)
> Oh no! Let us stop and think. (pause) Their things are missing. (pause) How do Tala and Bunso feel?
- check: They feel... → **worried**

### 10. Story: Before Going to School — story (Tala)
> Who will help them? (pause) Tatay calls, hurry up, or you will be late! (pause) Nanay says, I will help you look. (pause) They look here. (pause) They look there.

### 11. Story: Before Going to School — story (Tala)
> Will they find the pencil and crayons? (pause) And then. (pause) Look! (pause) Under the bed! (pause) There is the pencil. (pause) And there are the crayons! (pause) Thank you, Nanay!

### 12. Story: Before Going to School — story (Tala)
> Now everyone is happy. (pause) Nanay smiles. (pause) Next time, she says, (pause) put your things away before you sleep. (pause) Tala and Bunso say, yes, Nanay!

### 13. What Happened First? — order (Tala)
> That is the whole story! Can you remember it? (pause) What happened in the story? (pause) Tap the pictures in order. (pause) First. (pause) Next. (pause) Last.
- tap **They got ready.** → "First, they got ready for school."
- tap **Things were missing.** → "Next, the pencil and crayons were missing."
- tap **They found them!** → "Last, they found them under the bed!"

### 14. Think About the Story — pick (Tala)
> You remembered the order! Now let us think about the people. (pause) Let us think about the story.
- check: Who helped them look? → **Nanay**
- check: Where were the things? → **under the bed**

### 15. What do YOU do in the morning? — sentence (Tala)
> Tala and Bunso got ready for school. What about you? (pause) Now, let us talk about you! (pause) What do you do in the morning? (pause) Tap one. Then say it with me.
- tap **wake up** → "In the morning, I wake up."
- tap **take a bath** → "In the morning, I take a bath."
- tap **eat breakfast** → "In the morning, I eat breakfast."
- tap **brush my teeth** → "In the morning, I brush my teeth."

### 16. Your Turn to Talk! — talk (Tala)
> Now say it all by yourself! (pause) Tell me about your morning. (pause) Say: First, I wake up. (pause) Next, I eat breakfast. (pause) Your turn!

### 17. verse — verse (Tala)
> Nanay and Tatay helped Tala and Bunso. The Bible tells us how to treat them. (pause) Our Bible verse is, (pause) Honor your father and your mother. (pause) Honor means we listen to them. (pause) And we say thank you, like Tala and Bunso!

### 18. You Are Ready for School! — celebrate (Tala)
> You worked so hard today! (pause) Hooray! (pause) You learned five morning words. (pause) You told the story in order. (pause) And you talked about your morning. (pause) See you tomorrow!
