# Songs and Stories

Grade 1 English · Quarter 1 • Week 1 • Day 3 · `g1q1w1d3`

## Objectives (the child will...)

1. Sing and name vegetables
2. Sing and move my body
3. Listen to two stories

Source: LANG1Q1W1DAY3NARRATIONSCRIPT — Nursery Rhymes and Stories (Exodus 20:12)

## Slides

### 1. Songs and Stories — cover (Tala)
> Good morning, my friend! (pause) Yesterday we got ready for school. (pause) Today we sing songs. (pause) And we listen to stories!

### 2. Bahay Kubo — story (Tala)
> Look at this little house. (pause) It is a bahay kubo. (pause) It is a small house. (pause) Look all around it. (pause) It has a big garden with many vegetables!

### 3. Vegetables in the Garden — cards (Tala)
> Let us meet some vegetables! (pause) Tap each one. (pause) Say its name with me.
- tap **pechay** → "Pechay. (pause) It has big green leaves!"
- tap **bawang** → "Bawang. (pause) In English, garlic. (pause) It is white!"
- tap **luya** → "Luya. (pause) In English, ginger. (pause) It is brown and bumpy!"

### 4. Let Us Sing: Bahay Kubo — chant (Tala)
> Now let us sing! (pause) Tap a line to hear it. (pause) Or tap the big button to sing it all. (pause) Listen for bawang and luya!
- tap **Bahay kubo, kahit munti,** → "Bahay kubo, kahit munti,"
- tap **ang halaman doon ay sari-sari.** → "ang halaman doon ay sari-sari."
- tap **Singkamas at talong, sigarilyas at mani,** → "Singkamas at talong, sigarilyas at mani,"
- tap **sibuyas, kamatis, bawang at luya!** → "sibuyas, kamatis, bawang at luya!"

### 5. Find the Vegetable! — pick (Tala)
> Can you find the vegetables? (pause) Listen. (pause) Then tap!
- check: Tap the garlic! → **bawang**
- check: Tap the ginger! → **luya**
- check: Tap the pechay! → **pechay**

### 6. Sing and Move! — act (Tala)
> Please stand up! (pause) Let us sing and move. (pause) Touch each part of your body with me!
- tap **Touch your feet!** → "Paa! (pause) Touch your feet!"
- tap **Touch your knees!** → "Tuhod! (pause) Touch your knees!"
- tap **Touch your shoulders!** → "Balikat! (pause) Touch your shoulders!"
- tap **Touch your head!** → "Ulo! (pause) Touch your head!"

### 7. The Turtle and the Monkey — story (Tala)
> Story time! (pause) This is Turtle. (pause) And this is Monkey. (pause) They find a banana plant. (pause) They cut it in two. (pause) Monkey takes the top. Turtle takes the bottom.

### 8. The Turtle and the Monkey — story (Tala)
> Monkey laughs at Turtle. (pause) But Turtle plants it. (pause) She waters it every day. (pause) It grows tall. (pause) It grows bananas! (pause) Turtle is kind and patient.

### 9. Think About the Story — pick (Tala)
> Let us think about the story.
- check: Who was patient? → **Turtle**

### 10. The Legend of the Pineapple — story (Tala)
> Here is another story. (pause) This is Pina. (pause) Her mother was sick. (pause) She asked Pina to help. (pause) But Pina said, I cannot find it! (pause) She did not even look.

### 11. The Legend of the Pineapple — story (Tala)
> The next day, Pina was gone. (pause) A new fruit grew in the garden. (pause) It had many, many eyes! (pause) It is the pineapple. (pause) The pinya!

### 12. What Does the Story Teach? — pick (Tala)
> What does the story teach us?
- check: What should we do at home? → **Help!**

### 13. Which story did you like? — sentence (Tala)
> Which story did you like? (pause) Tap one. (pause) Then say it with me.
- tap **turtle** → "I like the story about the turtle."
- tap **pineapple** → "I like the story about the pineapple."

### 14. verse — verse (Tala)
> Our Bible verse is, (pause) Honor your father and your mother. (pause) We honor them when we help at home.

### 15. Great Singing and Listening! — celebrate (Tala)
> Hooray! (pause) You sang Bahay Kubo. (pause) You moved your body. (pause) And you listened to two stories. (pause) See you tomorrow!
