### 1. Meeting Good Friends at School — cover (Tala)

### 2. 4 Friendship Words — cards (Tala)
> To make a friend, we need some special words. (pause) Here are four friendship words. (pause) Listen. (pause) Then tap each picture and say it with me!
- tap **friend** → "Friend. (pause) A friend is someone kind we play with."
- tap **smile** → "Smile. (pause) A smile is a happy face. (pause) Show me your smile!"
- tap **greet** → "Greet. (pause) When we greet, we say, Good morning!"
- tap **wave** → "Wave. (pause) We wave our hand to say hello."

### 3. Show Me! Be a Friend! — act (Tala)
> You know the words! Now let us practice being a friend. (pause) Stand up! (pause) I say it. (pause) You do it. (pause) Then tap the button.
- tap **Smile!** → "Smile! (pause) Show me a big happy face!"
- tap **Wave!** → "Wave! (pause) Hello, hello!"
- tap **Greet!** → "Greet! (pause) Say, Good morning!"
- tap **Say your name!** → "Point to yourself. (pause) Say, My name is, and your name!"

### 8. Story: Tala's New Friend — story (Tala)
> Then, Tala has an idea. (pause) Tala sees Lino. (pause) She smiles and waves. (pause) Good morning! My name is Tala. (pause) What is your name?

### 9. Story: Tala's New Friend — story (Tala)
> What does Lino say? (pause) Lino smiles. (pause) Good morning, Tala! My name is Lino. (pause) Tala says, do you want to draw with us? (pause) Yes! (pause) Now they are friends!

### 10. Think About the Story — pick (Tala)
> Tala made a new friend! (pause) Let us think about the story.
- check: Who is the new boy? → **Lino**
- check: What did Tala say? → **Good morning!**

### 11. What Happened First? — order (Tala)
> Can you remember the whole story? (pause) Tap the pictures in order. (pause) First. (pause) Next. (pause) Last.
- tap **Lino sat alone.** → "First, Lino sat alone."
- tap **Tala greeted Lino.** → "Next, Tala smiled and greeted Lino."
- tap **They drew together.** → "Last, they drew together. They are friends!"

### 12. How Do YOU Greet? — sentence (Tala)
> Tala greeted Lino. How do YOU greet a friend? (pause) How do you greet a friend? (pause) Tap one. (pause) Then say it with me.
- tap **Good morning!** → "I say, Good morning!"
- tap **Good afternoon!** → "I say, Good afternoon!"
- tap **Hello, friend!** → "I say, Hello, friend!"

### 13. Your Turn to Talk! — talk (Tala)
> Now make a new friend, just like Tala! (pause) Turn to a friend or a family member. (pause) Smile and wave. (pause) Say: Good morning! My name is Tala. (pause) Use your own name!
